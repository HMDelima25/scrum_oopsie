# Team Working Agreement

**Project:** HOPE ERP | **Sprint:** 1 | **Version:** 0.1 (draft for the team to review)


## 1. Why we have this
We agree on how we work together so that everyone knows what to expect, problems are raised early, and the work is shared fairly. We review this agreement at every sprint retrospective and change it when it is not working.

## 3. Sprint rhythm
Each sprint is 2 weeks. The project has 4 sprints (8 weeks).

| Meeting | When | How long | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint Planning | Day 1 of the sprint, [time] | 60 min | Agree on the sprint goal and who does which task |
| Daily standup | Every [weekday time] | 15 min max | Each person: what I did, what I will do, what blocks me |
| Mid-sprint check | Day 5, [time] | 20 min | Are we on track for the sprint goal? Adjust if needed |
| Sprint Review | Last day, [time] | 30 min | Show what is finished |
| Retrospective | Right after the review | 30 min | Improve how we work |

If you cannot attend, tell the team in [channel] **before** the meeting and post your standup update in writing.

## 4. Communication
- Main channel: **[Messenger group / Discord / Teams]**
- Task discussion happens in the **GitHub issue** for that task, so the history stays with the work.
- We reply to messages within **[24 hours]** on school days. If something is urgent, say "URGENT" and name the person.
- We are respectful and assume good intent. We criticize the work, not the person.

## 5. How we work in GitHub
1. One task = one issue = one branch = one pull request (PR).
2. Branch names: `feature/S1-DEV-02-short-name`, `docs/S1-SM-03-working-agreement`, `fix/short-name`.
3. Nobody commits directly to `main`. Everything goes through a PR.
4. Every PR has the line `Closes #<issue number>`, uses the PR template, and gets **at least 1 approval from a teammate** (not the author).
5. Reviewers respond within **[1 working day]**. Authors answer review comments within **[1 working day]**.
6. Work on at most **1 to 2 tasks at a time** (WIP limit). Finish before starting something new.
7. Everyone moves their own card on the board: Backlog, Ready, In progress, In review, Done. Update it before the daily standup.
8. We never commit passwords, tokens or other secrets to the repository.

## 6. Impediments (things that block us)
- Raise a blocker as soon as you see it, in the standup or in [channel]. Do not wait for the next meeting.
- The Scrum Master records it in the impediment log and follows up until it is resolved.
- If a blocker is not resolved in **[2 working days]**, the Scrum Master escalates it (for example to the instructor).

## 7. Decisions and disagreements
- We decide by discussing first, then by team consensus. If we cannot agree after a fair discussion, we vote and the majority decides.
- Technical decisions go in the GitHub issue or PR so we can find them later.
- If we disagree about the sprint scope, the Product Owner makes the final call on priorities, and the team decides how much it can commit to.

## 8. When someone cannot deliver
- Say it early. Telling the team on day 3 is far better than on day 9.
- The team decides together whether to get help, shrink the task, or move it to the next sprint.
- Nobody is blamed for raising a problem. Hidden problems are what hurt us.

## 9. Quality
We follow the Definition of Done in `docs/definition-of-done.md`. A task is not Done until it meets it.

## 10. Reviewing this agreement
We review this agreement at every retrospective. Any member can propose a change by opening a PR.
